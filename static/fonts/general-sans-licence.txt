-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT HAS TO BE PASTED HERE, AND BY WHOM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The full licence text is rendered by Fontshare's own site from a script, so it cannot be
fetched by curl: https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl returns a 691 byte shell that
says "Enable javascript to use this application". Three endpoints were probed
(www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl, api.fontshare.com/v2/license/itf-ffl,
api.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl) and only the first answers at all, as a shell.

Open that URL in a browser, copy the licence text, and replace this paragraph with it. The
licence is not a formality here: AGENT.md section 9 item 8 makes it the condition on which a
family may be used at all, on a site whose entire argument is that it does not take anything
on trust. A subset is a distribution of the font, so whatever terms apply have to travel
with the file, which is why this text sits next to the .woff2 files rather than in a note.

Until that is done, the family is in use on a licence whose text is not in the repository.
The maintainer supplied these files and prefers this family, which is why it is here; the
prose does not yet say on the site which licence General Sans is served under, and it must
not say so until this file can back the claim up.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE ITF OFFERS, AS THE PUBLISHER DESCRIBES IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fontshare is ITF's free font service. It divides its catalogue into "Open Source" families
under the SIL Open Font License and "Closed Source" families under its own free licence, and
General Sans is in the second group. The practical difference is that the ITF font software
itself stays ITF's: it may be used, including in commercial work, but it may not be
redistributed as a font or resold, and it may not be modified and passed off under the same
name. Those are second-hand summaries of the publisher's terms and are recorded here only so
the next reader knows what to check; they are not the licence.

For comparison, Proza Libre, the display family on this site, is under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1, whose full text is in proza-libre-OFL.txt beside it.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS DONE TO THE FILES, FOR THE LICENCE'S SAKE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subsetted, not modified: glyphs outside the site's character set were removed and the result
was converted to WOFF2 by scripts/build-fonts.py. Nothing was redrawn, renamed, or
re-encoded beyond that format conversion, and the family and style names inside the files are
the originals. The script records the exact command, and `python3 scripts/build-fonts.py`
rebuilds the same bytes from the library in fontok/.
